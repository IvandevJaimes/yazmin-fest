# Yazmin Fest Invitation

Scope: the single invitation page at `/`.

Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: invited guests opening the link on a phone to understand the celebration and decide whether to attend.

Action: confirm attendance by opening a prefilled WhatsApp message to either supplied contact. Directions and the gift alias support the guest after that decision. There is no form.

Evidence: the client supplied the event facts, eleven portrait photographs, and one short vertical video. The visible Instagram reel preview establishes a light botanical cover with centered “MIS XV” lettering and a script name; the rest of the reel was not available, so the implementation follows that visible direction and the client's facts.

Constraints: preserve the client's wording and supplied imagery; exclude beige and silver; do not invent the relationships between people in the photographs.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a personal botanical keepsake that unfolds into a useful invitation. It refuses generic rows of equal cards and puts the guest's next action inside the invitation itself.

OWN-WORLD: Bright white, berry, rose, and leaf green carry the surface. A high-contrast editorial face marks “MIS XV”; a fine handwritten face owns Yazmin's name. Supplied photographs stay natural and full-color. Details sit in open, print-like sections with thin botanical-green rules, never beige or silver panels.

STORY: Guests first recognize Yazmin and the date, then see both arrival times and the venue, browse real photos and a short video, and confirm through WhatsApp. The map and gift alias remain easy to find.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On a 390 × 844 phone, center “MIS XV” and the handwritten “Yazmin” over an open white field, with botanical photo material entering from the lower edge. Show 20 November and a direct WhatsApp confirmation action before the fold. On desktop, retain the invitation's vertical rhythm and give the portrait material more room without turning it into a split hero.

FORM: The user's pinned direction, ranked first: a vertical, centered botanical invitation like the visible reel cover, followed by a scrollable photo-story page. Seed reference:931923318cff002a.
