## Platform

web

## Users

Guests invited to Yazmin's 15th birthday celebration, opening the invitation from a shared link, primarily on a phone.

## Product Purpose

A personal digital invitation for Yazmin's 15th birthday. It helps guests understand when and where to arrive, learn when Yazmin will make her entrance, confirm attendance, and find the gift account alias.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One invitation page with event details and a prominent dress-code feature within that section, a countdown, a continuous photo mosaic, an embedded venue map, RSVP confirmation, and a copyable gift alias.
- RSVP opens an in-page name and surname dialog, then a prefilled WhatsApp message to either supplied number. Save the confirmation in local storage, offer a resend action, and do not add an external RSVP form.
- The gift account alias is `yazminsc18`.
- The supplied date is 20 November. The year is assumed to be 2026 because the project is being prepared on 27 October 2026; keep this easy to change if the client confirms another year.
- Guest arrival is at 22:00; Yazmin's entrance is at 23:00. The client supplied `23:000`, normalized here to `23:00`.
- Venue: Salón Isaura Eventos, Ayacucho 300, Alderetes.
- Dress code: elegant attire; guests should avoid beige and silver.
- RSVP contacts: 3816136054 and 3814907903.

## Brand Commitments

- The event title is “Yazmin Fest”; keep the name spelling supplied by the client.
- Use beige as the invitation's main surface, with dusty pastel rose, sage, cocoa, and restrained silver accents replacing the earlier plum palette.
- Follow the supplied Instagram reel reference for the invitation's presentation: https://www.instagram.com/p/DadKgw7AY6A/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&cplk=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==&srtk=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==

## Evidence on Hand

- Eleven portrait JPEG photographs and one MP4 supplied in `assets/`.
- The Instagram reference's public preview shows a centered “MIS XV” title and script name with botanical corner artwork and generous light negative space. The full reel itself was not available to the browser during implementation.
- No verified names or relationships for people appearing in the photographs; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Make the date, venue, and two arrival times easy to find.
- Tell guests to dress elegantly and avoid beige and silver.
- Let guests confirm by sending a WhatsApp message in one tap.
- Keep Yazmin's supplied photographs and event details as the source of truth.
